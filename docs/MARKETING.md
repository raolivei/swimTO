## Social Media Copy Templates

### Reddit (r/toronto, r/askTO)

**Important:** Follow subreddit rules. Don't spam. Be helpful, not promotional.

**Title options:**
- "Made a free tool to find drop-in swim times across Toronto"
- "Free website to check lane swim schedules at Toronto community centers"

**Post body:**
```
Hey Toronto swimmers!

I built a free tool to make finding drop-in swim times easier. It pulls data from the City of Toronto Open Data and shows lane swim, recreational swim, and adult swim schedules at 50+ community centers.

Features:
- Interactive map to find pools near you
- Filter by swim type (lane swim, rec swim, etc.)
- Updated daily from official city data
- Works on mobile, no app download needed

Check it out: https://swimto.app

Would love feedback from fellow swimmers. What features would make this more useful for you?
```

## Partnership Outreach Templates

### Swim Clubs / Masters Swimming Groups

**Email subject:** Free tool for your members - Toronto pool schedules

**Email body:**
```
Hi [Club Name],

I'm a Toronto swimmer who built a free tool called SwimTO (swimto.app) 
that shows drop-in swim schedules at community centers across the city.

I thought your members might find it useful for:
- Finding lane swim times near them
- Checking schedules when their regular pool is closed
- Discovering new pools to try

The data comes from the City of Toronto Open Data and updates daily.

Would you be open to sharing it with your members? Happy to answer 
any questions.

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Target Locations for Flyers

Priority community centers (high foot traffic):
1. Your local pool (start here)
2. Pools you visit regularly
3. Large community centers with multiple programs

**Tips:**
- Ask permission before posting
- Check bulletin board policies
- Replace weathered flyers periodically

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Be helpful and genuine
- Respond to questions and feedback
- Focus on value to users
- Ask permission for flyers

**Don't:**
- Spam multiple subreddits at once
- Post the same content repeatedly
- Be pushy or overly promotional
- Ignore subreddit/group rules
